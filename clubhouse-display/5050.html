Doing a 50/50 draw

Preview. The sign isn't built yet. This shows how it will work.

There are two ways to draw: the sign draws the winning ticket at random, or a person draws a stub from the bucket as usual and the sign shows the number. Pick one for each event. The host runs the sign with the button near the microphone.

1. When sales close

The ticket seller emails the sign the winner's prize. If the sign is drawing, the email also gives the first and last number of each roll of tickets handed out, including any strips torn off but not sold. The sender's email address must be approved to run 50/50 draws. As soon as the email arrives, the sign shows the prize (and turns itself on if it was off):

50/50: $54 for winner

The sign replies with how many tickets there are. If two rolls have tickets ending in the same 4 digits, the reply warns you to call out the whole number.

2a. If the sign draws

Bring the unsold strips to the front and give them to the person at the microphone, or lay them on the counter where everyone can see.

Press the button. Like a slot machine, every digit of the ticket number spins, then they stop one at a time from left to right: the first ones quickly, the last few slower and slower, with a 4-second wait before the very last digit. The digits before the last 4 are smaller, so the last 4 are easy to read out:

Ticket 123456

Not sold, or winner not here? If the number is on one of the unsold strips, or nobody has it, press again. The sign shows "Drawing again" and the digits spin again; say over the microphone why. The sign never draws the same ticket twice, so every ticket sold has the same chance. Presses while the digits are spinning are ignored. Whether the winner must be present is up to the club.

2b. If a person draws

Draw a stub from the bucket and read the number out right away, as usual. Then send it with the 50/50 winner form, which only needs the number (have it open on your phone before you draw). The sign shows it when the email arrives, usually within half a minute, for anyone who didn't catch it:

Ticket 123456

Only the person who set up the draw, or the person they named in the setup email, can send the number, and only while the draw is on the sign. Anyone else's email is refused, and the person who set it up gets a copy of every number sent.

Winner not here? Draw again, read it out, and send the new number; it replaces the first.

3. Finish

Hold the button for about 2 seconds, and the sign goes back to showing events. If nobody does, it goes back by itself after 3 hours.
