Mockup · Golden Gate clubhouse

Clubhouse sign over the kitchen opening

This is how the LED sign would look, using events from the October 29 weekly email. Use the Layout menu to compare showing one message at a time (each event, and the announcement, gets the whole sign for a few seconds) with two lines at once (events scroll along the top while the announcement stays on the bottom). Events disappear by themselves once their day has passed.

About 63 in × 12.6 in · 320 × 64 lights (5 × 2 panels) · on 7 am to 11 pm

Try the 50/50 draw

The host emails the sign the ticket rolls handed out and the prize, then presses the button by the microphone to draw. Here these buttons stand in for the button. Each draw spins every digit; they stop left to right, slower toward the end. The digits before the last 4 are drawn smaller.
