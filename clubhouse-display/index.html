The clubhouse sign

Preview. The sign isn't built yet. These pages show how it will work.

The sign above the kitchen opening shows the park's upcoming events and announcements, one message at a time: the date and time on top, the event's name below. Every few events it shows an announcement.

  • Events and announcementsWhere they come from, and how club officers change them
  • Picking potluck tablesThe sign calls the tables in a random order
  • Doing a 50/50 drawThe sign draws the winning ticket
  • Something's wrong with the signDark, stuck or showing the wrong thing

Turning the sign off during events

A doorbell button near the microphone at the end of the counter turns the sign off, for example during a speech, a show or a movie. Press it once to turn the sign off, and again to turn it back on. If nobody turns it back on, it comes back on by itself after 3 hours.

For an event planned ahead, a club officer can also set the sign to be off by email.

During a potluck or a 50/50 draw, the button runs those instead.

At night

The sign turns itself off overnight and back on in the morning.
