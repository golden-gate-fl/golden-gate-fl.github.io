The clubhouse sign

Preview. The sign isn't built yet. This page shows how it will work; the email address on the forms is a placeholder, so emails sent from them will bounce.

The sign above the kitchen opening shows the park's upcoming events and announcements, one message at a time: the date and time on top, the event's name below. Every few events it shows an announcement.

  • Change the signFor club officers: announcements, events, potluck and 50/50
  • Something's wrong with the signDark, stuck or showing the wrong thing

Where the events come from

The sign reads the park's weekly update email from West Coast Management by itself. Every event in the update goes on the sign, and past events drop off on their own. Each new update replaces everything from the last one.

To get an event or notice on the sign, have it put in the weekly update. That's the only way it stays there.

Changing it by email

Club officers can add an announcement, fix an event or turn the sign off for an evening by emailing the sign. Only approved email addresses can change it. The sign replies to say exactly what it will show, and how to undo the change.

Changes made by email last until the next weekly update, which replaces them.

The forms write the email for you: fill one in, press the button, and your mail app opens with the email ready to send.

The button by the microphone

A doorbell button near the microphone at the end of the counter turns the sign off, for example during a speech or a movie. Press it again to turn the sign back on. It comes back on by itself after 3 hours.

During a potluck or a 50/50 draw, the button runs those instead (the host is told how when they set it up).

At night

The sign turns itself off overnight and back on in the morning.
