Something's wrong with the printer

What happenedTry this
No reply at all, after 10 minutesThe printer only answers people on its print list. Check that you sent it from the address on the print list. If you did, ask a Men's Club officer.
The reply says it printed, but nothing came outLook on top of the printer and around it. If it's out of paper, tell the park office.
The reply says "not finished yet"The printer may be off, out of paper or jammed. Check that it's turned on. If it's out of paper or jammed, tell the park office. Once it's fixed, wait a few minutes: the job may still print. If it doesn't, send the file again.
Out of paper, pages faint or streaky (toner), paper jam, or the printer seems brokenTell the park office.
A long PDF didn't printPDFs over 10 pages aren't printed. Send only the pages you need.
A Word or other file didn't printSave it as a PDF first. See what it can print.

Please don't unplug the small computer plugged into the printer: it's what reads the email. If you think it needs restarting, ask a Men's Club officer.

Who to tell

  • The printer itself (paper, toner, jams, or it's broken): the park office.
  • Printing by email (getting on the print list, or no reply): a Men's Club officer.

For the printer's maintainers

Full maintenance notes, parts and how to set it up again are on the printer's maintenance page (needs a GitHub account with access).
