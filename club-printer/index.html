Print by email at the clubhouse

Any park resident can print on the Men's Club printer at the clubhouse from a phone, tablet or computer: email the file to the printer, and it prints in a minute or two. Then you get an email back saying whether it printed.

First, get on the print list: ask a Men's Club officer to add your email address. You only need to do this once.

Start an email to the printer Opens your email app. Attach your file, then press Send.

The printer's address:

GGmensclub+print@gmail.com

If the button doesn't open your email, copy the address and start an email to it yourself.

How to print

  1. Send it from your own email address, the one on the print list. Mail from any other address is ignored.
  2. Attach the file (the paper clip in most email apps). You can attach more than one. The subject and the message don't matter.
  3. Press Send. The printer checks for email every minute, so your pages are usually waiting by the time you get to it.
  4. Watch for the reply from GGmensclub@gmail.com. It lists each file and whether it printed.

What it can print

  • PDF files, up to 10 pages each. A longer PDF isn't printed at all; the reply tells you how many pages it has. Send only the pages you need.
  • Photos and pictures (JPG and PNG), one page each, made to fit the page.
  • Printing is black and white.

Word, Excel or PowerPoint files can't be printed. Save them as a PDF first, then attach the PDF: in most programs, choose File, then Save As or Export, and pick PDF. On a phone, a web page or document can usually be saved as a PDF from Share or Print.

Small pictures that are part of an email, such as a logo in your signature, aren't printed. Photos you attach or paste in are.

What the reply means

printed (3 pages)

It printed. Pick it up at the printer.

NOT printed: it has 25 pages, and the limit is 10 pages per file

Send a shorter PDF, or only the pages you need.

NOT printed: not a PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG file, skipped

Save it as a PDF and send it again.

not finished yet; the printer may be off, out of paper or jammed

See something's wrong with the printer.

No reply at all?

The printer only answers people on its print list, so it doesn't reply to mail from an address it doesn't know. Check that you sent it from the address on the print list. To get an address added, ask a Men's Club officer.

  • Something's wrong with the printerNothing came out, no reply, paper or toner. Printer problems go to the park office.

Your email and files

Your email and its attachments are kept in the Men's Club's email account, where the people who look after the printer can see them. Don't send anything you wouldn't want them to read.
